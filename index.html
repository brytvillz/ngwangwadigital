OUR STORY

Built from stubbornness.
Rooted in Nnewi.

NgwaNgwa Digital was born out of refusal — refusal to wait for opportunities that weren't coming, refusal to stop building when the capital ran out, refusal to let great skills go to waste.

Our founder started with a cohort program in Enugu, built a logistics MVP in 15 days, had an accident mid-sprint and kept going, then presented on stage at the Enugu Tech Fest. No investor showed up. But the mission became clear.

NgwaNgwa Digital exists to build real digital products and systems — and eventually, to create real opportunities for the skilled Nigerian developers who deserve better than an empty job market.
