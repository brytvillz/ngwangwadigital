OUR STORY

Built from stubbornness.
Rooted in Nnewi.

NgwaNgwa Digital was born out of refusal — refusal to wait for opportunities that weren't coming, refusal to stop building when the capital ran out, refusal to let great skills go to waste.

Our founder started with a cohort program in Enugu, built a logistics MVP in 15 days, had an accident mid-sprint and kept going, then presented on stage at the Enugu Tech Fest. No investor showed up. But the mission became clear.

NgwaNgwa Digital exists to build real digital products and systems — and eventually, to create real opportunities for the skilled Nigerian developers who deserve better than an empty job market.

THE JOURNEY

NgwaNgwa Delivery

The product that started everything. Six slides. One real story.

01 / 06

3MTT Impact Summit — The Spark

I was a 3MTT Fellow on the AI/ML track — completed all fundamental courses and got certified. At the Impact Summit Hackathon, surrounded by builders and badge lanyards, the question hit me differently: why not build something that solves a real Nigerian problem? That day, NgwaNgwa Delivery was born.

02 / 06

5 Days In. Accident. Kept Going.

Five days into the build I had an accident. My hand was bandaged. I kept coding. This photo is from that day — the NgwaNgwa Delivery app on screen, my wrapped hand on the keyboard. Nobody was going to build this for me, so I built it myself.

03 / 06

On Stage at Enugu Tech Fest

I showed up at the Enugu Tech Fest with what I'd built and asked to present. I got the chance. Standing on that stage, holding a microphone, showing a real product built from scratch — that moment changed my understanding of what I was capable of.

04 / 06

Listening to Real People

I sat with vendors and riders — not to pitch, but to understand. A logistics manager walked me through the chaos. A rider explained why trust breaks down on every delivery. Both sides carry real pain. I recorded it all. The app was built from those conversations, not assumptions.

🚚 Image coming soon

05 / 06

3 Days. Real Operations.

We ran a live pilot. Riders came on board. Vendors used the platform. The idea worked in the real world. But without capital to sustain operations, we had to pause. Zero sales — not because the product failed, but because the funding wasn't there yet.

🌱 Image coming soon

06 / 06

The Pivot — Not the End

NgwaNgwa Delivery is not dead. We pivoted to NgwaNgwa Digital to generate the capital needed to fund logistics operations properly. Every website we build, every product we ship — it's all moving toward the moment we relaunch this.

THE VISION IS ALIVE

NgwaNgwa Delivery is paused — not abandoned.

If you're a logistics company, investor, or partner who sees what this can become, we want that conversation.
