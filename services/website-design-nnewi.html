What you get with a website from us

Every website we build includes the following. These are not add-ons — they come standard because a website without them is not really useful.

  • Design built around your business — not a template we bought somewhere
  • Works on phones, tablets, and computers without breaking
  • Fast enough to load on a typical Nigerian mobile connection
  • Contact form or WhatsApp button so customers can reach you directly
  • Google Maps embed showing your physical location
  • Basic SEO setup — your business name and location in the right places so Google can find you
  • Your domain connected and SSL certificate set up (the padlock that shows a site is secure)
  • One month of free support after we hand over the site

If you already have a site that is outdated or not working well on mobile, we can rebuild it from scratch. Bring whatever you have — or nothing at all.

Who this is for

If you run a business in Nnewi — or you supply to businesses here, export from here, or serve customers here — a proper website will help you. Specifically:

  • Shops, restaurants, and service providers who want customers to find them on Google
  • Manufacturers and suppliers who need to send a link to buyers instead of a PDF or photos on WhatsApp
  • Professionals — lawyers, accountants, consultants — who want to look credible before a client meeting
  • Anyone who has been told "send me your website" and had nothing to send

If you are not sure whether you need a website or just a Google Business Profile, message us. We will tell you honestly which one makes more sense for where you are right now.

How long does it take

A standard business website takes two to three weeks from the day we receive your content. Here is what that looks like:

Week 1
We meet (on WhatsApp or in person), you tell us about your business, and we agree on what the site needs. You send us your logo, photos, and any text you already have.
Week 2
We build the site and send you a preview link. You review it and tell us what to change. We make the changes.
Week 3
Final review, your domain connected, site goes live. We walk you through what you own and how to reach us if something needs updating.

If you do not have content ready — no photos, no logo, no copy — that is fine, but it will add time. We can help you put together what you need.

What does it cost

Starting from ₦150,000

This covers a standard business website of up to five pages: Home, About, Services, Contact, and one extra. Larger sites, e-commerce, or custom systems are quoted separately based on what you need.

We do not charge a monthly fee just to keep your site online. Hosting is a separate cost — typically around ₦20,000–₦30,000 per year depending on which provider we use for you.

We ask for 50% upfront and 50% when the site is ready to go live. No payment means no work starts — that is fair on both sides.

Why us and not someone else

We are based in Nnewi. If something goes wrong, you can reach us — not a developer who is three states away and does not pick up. We built this agency to serve businesses here, so we understand the market, the customers, and the context.

We also built our own product — Virtual Waitress, a restaurant operations system now in use in Nnewi — so you can see what our own work looks like before you hire us for yours.
