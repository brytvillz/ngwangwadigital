Anambra and the Southeast produce more manufactured goods than any other region in Nigeria. Auto parts, packaging, building materials, plastic products, textiles — most made right here. The challenge isn't production. It's being found.

THE REAL PROBLEMS

Great products. Not enough visibility where buyers are looking.

Manufacturers don't need to go viral. They need to be verifiable — the moment a buyer Googles your business, they should find exactly what they need to say yes.

International buyers can't verify your credibility online

A buyer in Lagos, London, or Dubai searches your company name. They find nothing — or an outdated listing. Without a professional web presence, you lose the deal before the conversation starts.

No digital product catalogue or spec sheets

Your products exist. Your quality is real. But buyers can't see it unless they visit your factory. A digital catalogue with product specs, images, and MOQs changes what a buyer can do before they ever call you.

You're losing bids to competitors with better websites

A competitor who manufactures the same product as you is winning the tender — not because their product is better, but because their website looked more credible. Perception closes deals your product should be winning.

Dealer and distributor relationships are hard to manage at scale

Your distributors call your sales team for price lists, availability, and order updates. A dealer portal automates this — they log in, see real-time information, and place orders without a phone call.
