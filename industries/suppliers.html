You already know what's limiting your growth.

You sell to the same 30 clients

Your current clients are loyal. But your growth is limited by who already knows you. There's no channel for new buyers to find you except through referrals — and referrals don't scale.

International buyers can't find you

Buyers in the UK diaspora, and procurement officers at international companies, search Google first. If you don't have a professional web presence, you simply don't exist to them — no matter how good your product is.

Your catalogue is outdated or inaccessible

A 3-year-old PDF forwarded over WhatsApp is not a catalogue. Buyers need current pricing, current stock, current specifications — available instantly without calling your sales team for every inquiry.

Trade shows cost too much to be your only channel

You spend weeks preparing and thousands of naira attending trade fairs to meet buyers you'll see once. Your website should do what a trade show does — every day, for free, at scale.

HOW TECHNOLOGY FIXES IT

What we build for suppliers
and distributors.

For new buyer discovery

Professional Product Catalogue Website

A clean, mobile-first website that showcases your full product range with photos, specifications, and pricing. Organised by category. Optimised for Google. Buyers in any state — or any country — can browse your entire range and send an inquiry in seconds.

New buyers find you without knowing your name first

For Google visibility

Search Optimisation for Product & Location Keywords

We optimise your website so you rank when buyers search your specific product category and location — "kitchen equipment supplier Lagos," "FMCG distributor Nigeria," "packaging supplier Anambra." The right buyers find you at the moment they're ready to buy.

Rank for your exact product and location
Consistent inbound leads from Google
Reach buyers who are actively looking
For inquiry management

WhatsApp Quote Request System

Every product page on your website has a "Request a Quote" button that opens a WhatsApp message pre-filled with the product name and buyer's contact. You receive structured inquiries directly on your phone. No missed calls. No lost leads from generic contact forms.

Every inquiry goes directly to your sales team
Structured messages — product name, quantity, contact
Faster response = faster close
For repeat business

B2B Buyer Portal

For high-volume distributors with regular clients: a private portal where your buyers log in, view current stock and pricing, place orders, and track status. Reduces the back-and-forth WhatsApp chains for every transaction. Your sales team handles exceptions, not routine orders.

Regular buyers can order without calling you
Fewer errors from manual order-taking
