THE REAL PROBLEMS

You already know what's not working.

Most restaurant technology problems aren't solved by buying an expensive POS system. They're solved by making your existing process smarter.

Orders get lost or wrong

Staff mishear. They miswrite. Customers wait. Tables get annoyed. The same order errors happen every service, every week.

Customers wait too long just to order

They sit down, look around, wave their hand. Your staff is busy with another table. The experience starts badly before the food even arrives.

Your menu isn't online

People search for restaurants on Google before they leave the house. If your menu isn't there, they pick the competitor whose menu they can already see.

No way to reach customers again

A guest has a great meal and leaves. You have no way to reach them for the next visit. Every table is a stranger you never see twice.

HOW TECHNOLOGY FIXES IT

From problem to system.
Here's what we build.

For order errors

QR Menu + Digital Ordering

Customers scan a QR code on their table, browse your menu on their phone, and place their order digitally. No misheard items. No handwriting errors. The order goes straight to your kitchen display or WhatsApp.

Fewer errors per service
Staff free to focus on real hospitality
Works on any phone — no app required
For long wait times

Digital Waiter Call System

A button on your QR menu lets customers call the waiter digitally — a notification goes straight to staff. No waving, no shouting, no ignored customers. Your team sees the request and responds.

Faster table service without more staff
Customers feel attended to, even at peak hours
More tables turned per service
For online visibility

Menu Website + Google Business

We build a clean, mobile-first menu page for your restaurant and set up your Google Business profile so you appear when people search "restaurants near me" or your restaurant's name. Sharable link, indexed by Google.

Shows up on Google Maps and search
Customers see your menu before they arrive
Shareable link for Instagram and WhatsApp
For repeat customers

WhatsApp Follow-Up Flow

After a customer visits, a simple WhatsApp flow lets you collect feedback, send a promotion, or just say thank you. Build the database of customers you can actually reach again.

Build a direct line to your loyal customers
Promote specials, new menus, and events
Turn one-time visitors into regulars
