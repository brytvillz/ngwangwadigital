Nnewi is the auto parts capital of West Africa. But most of the businesses here have no digital presence. Their buyers are still calling, visiting physically, or going to dealers who showed up on Google first. The market is already digital. The question is whether your business is ready for it.

THE REAL PROBLEMS

You already know what's limiting your reach.

Buyers can't see your stock remotely

Customers call from Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt — but without a digital catalogue, they have to travel to Nnewi or describe parts over the phone. You lose deals to dealers they can browse online.

Your competitor got a website and stole your customers

A nearby dealer set up a basic website and now they rank on Google for "auto parts Nnewi" and "spare parts Anambra." Their phone rings more than yours. The difference is digital visibility.

Your entire catalogue is in your head

You know your inventory. Your regular buyers know what you stock. But new buyers — or buyers from other states — have no way to discover what you carry without calling or visiting.

Word-of-mouth only reaches so far

You have loyal customers who refer you. But every referral is a one-to-one handoff. A digital presence turns every satisfied customer into a permanent recommendation that anyone can find.

HOW TECHNOLOGY FIXES IT

Practical solutions.
No technical jargon.

For buyers who can't see your stock

Digital Parts Catalogue

A professional, mobile-first website that showcases what you stock — organised by category, searchable, with photos and prices. Buyers in Lagos can browse your inventory at 9pm without calling you. You wake up to inquiries.

Buyers in any state can browse your inventory
Reduces time spent answering "do you have X?" calls
Shareable link — send to buyers on WhatsApp
For Google visibility

Google Business Setup & Local SEO

We set up your Google Business profile correctly and optimise your online presence so you rank when someone searches "auto parts Nnewi," "spare parts Anambra," or your specific product type. The phone calls come to you.

Appear on Google Maps for your area
Rank for the exact terms your buyers search
Get calls from buyers you've never met before
For taking orders

WhatsApp Ordering System

A buyer sees a part on your catalogue, taps a button, and an inquiry comes straight to your WhatsApp — with the part name, quantity, and their contact. You reply, confirm, and close the sale. No missed calls. No confusion.

Every inquiry goes directly to your phone
Structured messages — not random texts
Works on any smartphone, no new system to learn
For high-volume distributors

B2B Buyer Portal

If you supply to dealers in multiple states, a private portal lets your regular buyers log in, see current availability, place bulk orders, and track deliveries. No more back-and-forth over WhatsApp for every transaction.

Dealers can order without calling you first
Reduces order errors and double-bookings
Scalable as your distribution grows
