THE REAL PROBLEMS

Referrals only take you so far. Google takes you further.

Most professionals in Nigeria rely entirely on word-of-mouth. It works — until growth stalls, a key referrer leaves, or a new competitor with a better online presence starts taking your clients.

When someone Googles you, nothing comes up

A potential client hears your name, Googles you, and finds nothing — or worse, finds a competitor. That moment of doubt is enough to lose the inquiry. A proper website and Google listing prevent it.

Booking and scheduling still happens by phone or WhatsApp

You take calls, confirm via WhatsApp, and still get cancellations because the appointment wasn't tracked anywhere formal. An online booking page removes the back-and-forth and reduces no-shows.

Nothing to share when you meet a potential client

You meet someone at a function, they ask for your card — but what they really want is a link they can send their colleague. A professional website is something shareable, searchable, and impressive at the same time.

You don't appear on Google Maps

People search for "lawyer in Awka" or "consultant near me" and you're invisible. The professionals who set up their Google Business profile three years ago are getting those calls. You should be too.

HOW TECHNOLOGY FIXES IT

What we build for professional practices.

For credibility and trust

Professional Credibility Website

A clean, fast, mobile-first website built around your expertise — who you are, what you do, who you serve, and how to reach you. Not a template. Not a portfolio with stock photos. A site that makes the person landing on it think: "This is exactly who I need to call."

First impression matches your actual credibility
Shareable link for referrals and networking
Positions you above competitors without a web presence
For scheduling and conversions

Appointment Booking System

A booking page where prospects choose their consultation type, pick a time slot, and confirm — all without calling. The appointment goes to your WhatsApp and email. No double-bookings. No missed calls. No back-and-forth to find a free time.

Prospects book themselves — no phone tag
Calendar stays full, cancellations go down
Works while you're with another client
For Google visibility

Google Business & Local Search Setup

We set up and fully optimise your Google Business profile so your practice appears in Google Maps and local results for searches your clients actually use — "lawyer in Onitsha," "business consultant Enugu," "accountant near me." Show up where decisions are made.

Appear on Google Maps when prospects search locally
Verified listing builds instant trust
Inbound inquiries from searches you're not currently winning
For intake and follow-up

WhatsApp Contact & Intake System

A smart contact form on your website that sends inquiries directly to your WhatsApp — pre-formatted with the client's name, query type, and preferred contact time. So when a prospect submits a form, you receive a structured WhatsApp message instead of an email you might miss.

Inquiries come to where you actually check
Structured context before the first call
Faster response = more converted leads
