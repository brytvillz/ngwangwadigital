THE REAL PROBLEMS

Guests are booking digitally. Most Nigerian hotels aren't ready.

You don't need a complex hotel management system. You need the right digital touchpoints — and we know which ones actually move the needle.

Booking is still done by phone call

Guests who want to book have to call. If your line is busy, they call the next hotel. Every missed call is a missed booking. Online booking removes this friction entirely.

You don't show up when people search for hotels

When someone searches "hotels in [your city]," your hotel doesn't appear on Google Maps or in results. The bookings go to competitors who set up their Google presence years ago.

In-room service requests are slow

Guests call reception for room service, extra towels, or maintenance. The call gets missed or delayed. A QR-based request system makes service instant and trackable.

No system for collecting guest reviews

Happy guests leave without being asked to review. You lose the social proof that would convince the next guest to choose you. A post-stay WhatsApp message changes this.

HOW TECHNOLOGY FIXES IT

What we build for hotels.

For booking friction

Online Booking System

A simple booking page on your hotel website — room types, availability, pricing, and a "Book Now" button that opens a WhatsApp chat with the booking details pre-filled. No complex PMS needed. Guests book. You confirm. Done.

Guests book without calling — anytime, any day
Inquiry goes straight to your phone via WhatsApp
Reduce missed bookings from busy phone lines
For Google visibility

Google Business & Local SEO

We optimise your Google Business profile so your hotel appears in Google Maps and local search results when guests search for hotels in your city. Photos, reviews, pricing, amenities — all set up correctly so guests find you and choose you.

Appear on Google Maps for hotel searches
More direct bookings, less reliance on booking platforms
Consistent online presence that works 24/7
For in-room experience

QR Room Service & Digital Requests

We can build a QR-based in-room request system for your hotel — room service, housekeeping requests, maintenance reports — using the same platform as Virtual Waitress. A QR code in each room, and guests can request what they need without calling reception. No special hardware required.

Guests request service without calling reception
Staff see structured requests as they come in
Faster service, fewer missed requests
For reputation building

Post-Stay Review Collection

A simple WhatsApp message sent after checkout — thanking the guest and asking for a Google review. One click for the guest. One more five-star review for you. We set up the flow and the automated follow-up system so it runs every time without you thinking about it.

More Google reviews without manual chasing
Better review score = more organic bookings
Build a direct database of past guests
